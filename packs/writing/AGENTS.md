# Writing pack

Research, brainstorming, voice editing, teaching, and questionnaires.

## Skills

- `research` — research a question against primary sources and persist the findings as a numbered artifact.
- `brainstorming` — explore intent, requirements, and design before implementation; hands off to the `engineering` pack's `writing-plans` for well-specified follow-through.
- `humanize` — remove AI writing patterns and restore natural human voice.
- `teach` — a stateful, multi-session teaching workspace for one topic: mission, curated resources, lessons, references, learning records.
- `to-questionnaire` — turn a decision you can't fully answer into a structured questionnaire for someone else to fill in.
